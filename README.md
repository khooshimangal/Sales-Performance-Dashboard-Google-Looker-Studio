## Project Overview
4-page interactive business intelligence dashboard analysing 
Superstore retail sales data across revenue trends, regional 
performance, customer segments, and shipping behaviour.

## Tools Used
- **BI Tool:** Google Looker Studio
- **Dataset:** Sample Superstore (9,994 orders, 2014–2017)
- **Data Cleaning:** Resolved date format issues using 
  PARSE_DATE calculated field

## Dashboard Pages

| Page | Focus | Key Visual |
|---|---|---|
| Executive Overview | Overall business KPIs | Monthly trend + Category bar |
| Regional & Product | Geography and sub-category | Region Sales vs Profit |
| Customer Segments | Segment breakdown | Donut + Segment comparison |
| Shipping & Discount | Operations analysis | Scatter: Discount vs Profit |

## Key Findings
- **Technology leads** all categories in revenue at ~$836K
- **West region** is the highest performing region in both 
  sales ($725K) and profit
- **Central region** shows a significant margin gap — 
  high sales but disproportionately low profit
- **Consumer segment** drives 50.6% of total revenue
- **Higher discounts directly erode profit** — sub-categories 
  with heavy discounting show negative profit margins
- **Standard Class** handles 59% of all shipments
